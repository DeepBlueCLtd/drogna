# A title that is the finding, not the subject

Two or three paragraphs on the problem, in terms a reader outside the project would
recognise. No component names yet, no requirement numbers, nothing that assumes the
reader has seen the repository. If the problem is a question, ask it.

The second paragraph usually says why the obvious answer does not work, which is what
makes the rest of the entry worth reading.

<!-- more -->

## A heading that is a sentence about what happened

The body. One idea per section, and section headings that say what the section
concludes rather than what it is about — "The test that isolated nothing" rather than
"Testing". Link the first use of a term that needs it:
[decorrelation timescale](../../glossary.md#decorrelation-timescale).

## What did not work

The wrong turning, and what it looked like from the inside before it was recognised as
wrong. This section is not optional decoration; it is usually the reason the entry
exists.

## What is now known

One or two sentences, plainly. What is true now that was not known before, and what is
still not known — including any check that has not yet had the opportunity to fail.
