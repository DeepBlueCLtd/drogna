What changed, and the one thing it costs

The second pass drew thirteen components and fifteen wires, and the panel was taller than a laptop screen. This one groups them: sensing is one box, not two; measurement in is one box, not three; the whole forecast loop is one box carrying the four components that turn in it. Six steps instead of eleven.

The cost is a name that nothing declares. Every box in the last version was a component and carried that component's own label. A group's name — The forecast loop — is authored, so the storyboard now authors five things rather than four. What stops that from rotting is unchanged: each box lists the declared labels it stands for, the wires are still derived from the topology, and the gate still fails on any component that is in neither a group nor the recorded omissions.

Drawn light and flat on purpose, so it is not mistaken for the finished dark panel. Labels, membership and wires come from app/config/run.json and contracts/topology.json as they stand after today's merge — the analyst included.

    Still to settle

    1. Is six the right six? The grouping that carries the most weight is the loop: monitor, scheduler, analyst and runner in one box, with the internal ring — watch, decide, assimilate, forecast — told in prose rather than drawn. The two arrows to the fields are the cycle at this altitude.
    2. Do the member chips earn their place? Each box lists the declared labels it stands for, in small type. They are what keeps the abstraction anchored to the tree, but they are also the busiest thing left in the picture.
    3. The clock is gone, at your call, and is recorded in the omissions with its reason rather than dropped silently. The broker keeps the strip at the foot: it is what every message in the picture crosses, and it is the reason the wires are drawn publisher to subscriber instead of through one box. The release gate moved in with the query components, since it stands in front of every answer they give.
    4. Six steps, or five? The fields and the loop could be one step, told as "the loop turns against the store" — but the three eras are the reason the archive, the recent and the current can be talked about at all.
